## 3. Discrepancies & Audit Findings

### Finding 1: `TimezoneSelect.tsx` ARIA Controls Attribute
* **Location:** `artifacts/cadence/src/components/settings/TimezoneSelect.tsx:206`
* **Issue:** `aria-controls={listboxId}` is present on the input combobox even when the dropdown listbox is not mounted in the DOM (`isOpen === false`).
* **Impact:** Screen readers report an aria-controls target that does not exist until expanded.
* **Remediation:** Change to `aria-controls={isOpen ? listboxId : undefined}`.

### Finding 2: In-App Reduced Motion & Central Hook
* **Location:** `artifacts/cadence/src/components/shared/ActivityRings.tsx:3`
* **Issue:** `usePrefersReducedMotion()` is declared locally inside `ActivityRings.tsx`. No shared hook exists in `src/hooks/useReducedMotion.ts` or in `src/lib/`.
* **Impact:** Inability to toggle reduced motion in `/settings` or respect custom user preference across modals, toasts, and focus timer animations.
* **Remediation:**
  1. Extract `useReducedMotion()` to `artifacts/cadence/src/hooks/useReducedMotion.ts` supporting OS preference + `cadence_reduced_motion` in localStorage.
  2. Add an explicit "Reduced Motion" toggle in `/settings` under Accessibility.

### Finding 3: Density Modes Not Exposed in UI
* **Location:** `artifacts/cadence/src/components/task/TaskRow.tsx`
* **Issue:** `compact` density exists only as a prop on selected components, without a global display density context or settings selector.
* **Impact:** Users seeking high-density list views (Things 3 / Linear compact mode) cannot switch between `comfortable` (default 52px row) and `compact` (44px row).
* **Remediation:** Introduce a lightweight `DensityProvider` with `comfortable` / `compact` tokens.

### Finding 4: Disk Bundle Size vs First-Load Critical Path
* **Location:** `scripts/verify-web-vitals-budget.cjs`
* **Observation:**
  - Entry chunk: 100.31 kB gzip (pass against 120 kB).
  - Total JS on disk: 265.84 kB gzip (breaches 200 kB budget by 65.84 kB).
  - First-visit transfer: 189.82 kB JS / 220.46 kB total (passes 200 kB critical path standard).
* **Resolution:** Note is recorded as architectural finding per P26.2. No artificial budget loosening.
